Architectural Rationale Dual-Tier Bridging

By utilizing the RP2350 microcontroller for bare-metal scanning of all tactile buttons and optical quadrature pulses, the system guarantees deterministic sub-millisecond tactile latency, while the host engine natively delivers 60 FPS waveforms and CoreAudio DSP.

Engineering Documentation & Specifications
1. Tactile Control Surface

Preserves 100% of Pioneer tactile components: heavy dual-action optical jog wheel (quadrature pulses), 100mm ALPS 10kΩ pitch slider, microswitch Cue/Play matrix, and LED ring indicators.

2. RP2350 Microcontroller

Raspberry Pi Pico 2 runs bare-metal C firmware utilizing the TinyUSB stack. Hardware PIO state machines decode optical quadrature ticks with zero CPU overhead at 1,000 Hz polling rate.

3. CDJ-850 HID Emulation

The firmware broadcasts Pioneer Vendor ID 0x08E4 and Product ID 0x0159. Standard HID report packets mirror genuine Pioneer protocol for immediate native driver handshake.

4. Single-Deck Engine & Audio

Algoriddim djay running in Single-Deck Mode provides CDJ-3000 class moving multi-band waveforms, track search, key-matching, and streaming over Wi-Fi, outputting low-jitter stereo audio to rear RCA jacks.

All physical input/output interfaces, connectors, and cable assemblies required to build the player:

5 ESSENTIAL PORTS
PORT 1: Rear USB-C Passthrough (External) 12mm Panel Mount

Connector: IP67 Waterproof Panel-Mount USB-C Female Jack (with M12 locking nut).
Location: Rear metal backplate (single 12mm drill hole).
Function: Accepts external 5V/3A USB-PD charger or connects to a laptop for controller mode.

PORT 2: Pico 2 USB-HID Bridge (Internal) 1,000 Hz HID

Connector: Low-profile right-angle USB-C / Micro-USB jumper cable.
Location: Middle PETG suspension tray underneath screen.
Function: Transmits 1,000 Hz Pioneer CDJ-850 HID control packets directly to the iPod/iPad.

PORT 3: Pioneer 25-Pin FFC Flex Port (Internal) 1.0mm Pitch ZIF

Connector: 25-Pin 1.0mm pitch FPC/FFC bottom-contact ZIF breakout PCB.
Location: Front edge of lower chassis.
Function: Reads all Pioneer Play/Cue switches, jog optical quadrature pulses, and pitch fader.

PORT 4: Rear Gold-Plated RCA Audio Line-Out 24-Bit / 48kHz

Connector: Dual Gold-Plated RCA Female Jacks (Red = Right, White = Left).
Location: Rear metal backplate (original Pioneer factory opening).
Function: Connects directly into standard DJ mixers (e.g. Pioneer DJM-800/900).

PORT 5: Internal 5V DC Power Splitter / Hub Isolated Ground

Connector: USB-PD Power Splitter & 5V/3A DC step-down regulator.
Location: Lower chassis base.
Function: Powers Pico 2 (0.1W) and charges the iPod/iPad simultaneously without ground loop noise.
