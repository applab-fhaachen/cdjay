### Power Supply

What we we need: and I think that all comes from external, so we don't need to generate anything. (See Page 20)
V+7M 
V+8A
V+3D
V+6B
V+5P

On the regulator board, there are two ICs: IC92 and IC91. 
IC91: Takes V+6B and delivers V+3D
IC92: Takes V+10B and delivers V+7A
